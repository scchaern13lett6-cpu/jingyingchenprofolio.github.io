★ Project Overview · Filed 2025

Some rules protect you. Others watch you.

The horror isn't the supernatural. It's the architecture of erasure — the way institutions teach you to stop asking questions before you even know there were questions to ask.

Built entirely solo in Ren'Py 8.3.7, Under the University drops players into Harmon Valley University with a simple rulebook and an impossible task: survive three days. Some of the 13 campus rules are genuine survival protocols. Others are traps designed to look helpful. The game never tells you which is which.

Through branching narrative, sanity mechanics, and a clue-collection system, players uncover a buried 2003 hate crime — and the institutional silence built to hide it. The game is short, but it's not light. It asks players to feel the weight of small choices: trust the wrong rule, and you die. Trust the wrong person, and you live, but you don't know what you helped to bury.

What makes the horror work is not the scares. It's the uncertainty about authority. Every memo, every poster, every reassurance from a professor could be the truth or the trap. By day two, players stop reading the rules and start reading around them.

★ Investigation Board ★
01 · RULES

13 Campus Rules

Players receive 13 rules at the start. Some are genuine survival protocols. Others are traps. The game never tells you which is which — you have to figure it out by observation, paranoia, and the cost of being wrong.

02 · CLUES

Clue Collection

6 collectible documents — newspaper clippings, internal memos, autopsy reports — piece together what the university buried. Each clue rewards exploration but punishes wrong timing. Going to Building B at the wrong hour can end your run.

03 · SANITY

Sanity System

Witnessing anomalies and making wrong choices depletes sanity, distorting perception and eventually triggering hallucinations. The longer you play, the less you can trust what you see.

04 · ENDINGS

Multiple Endings

True Ending — expose the truth and escape. Silent Survival — leave without knowing. Death Endings — trust the wrong rule. Each ending reflects a different moral position on what survival is worth.

★ DESIGN INTENT

Why these four mechanics?

Each system is designed to weaponize trust. Rules teach you to obey. Clues teach you to seek. Sanity teaches you to doubt yourself. Endings teach you that knowing the truth costs something. Together, they recreate the experience of being inside an institution that does not want you to look too closely.

Designer's Statement

This project explores how game mechanics can carry serious social themes. The central mystery — a 2003 hate crime systematically concealed by the institution — is not backdrop. It is the point.

Six suspects. Or six victims.

Every face at Harmon Valley is a question. The dossiers tell you what they want you to know — not what you need to.

★ The Build

Built solo, from scratch.

Every system in Under the University was designed and coded as the sole developer. The horror demanded careful state management — branching narratives that remember every choice, sanity values that distort what players see, and a clue system that punishes shortcuts as much as it rewards exploration.
